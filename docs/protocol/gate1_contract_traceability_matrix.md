## Acceptance evidence contract

Every acceptance test named here records its decision ID, contract digest, authority identity, fixture or real-input identity, mutation, expected view/schema decision, expected status or stable failure code, call evidence, and proof/manifest digest. A passed fixture test does not authorize Gate 1X; readiness and publication gates must also pass.
